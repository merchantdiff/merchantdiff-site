IP allowlists are now required for custom apps installed outside your organization

Dev DashboardUpdate

Shopify developer change

MerchantDiff detected this entry in Shopify's official developer changelog on November 11, 2026.

This Shopify update is categorized as Dev Dashboard, Update.

What changed

We're enhancing protection of merchants by requiring IP allowlists for custom apps, if those apps are installed on stores outside of the organization that owns them. Starting November 20, 2026 at 11:59 PM ET: Apps must have an IP allowlist for new installs. Starting January 1, 2027 at 11:59 PM ET: Allowlists are required for existing installs. What apps are affected Custom apps installed on stores outside the organization that owns the app. Partners who own multiple apps.

Who is affected

Shopify app developers using the API or platform feature named in this update should review the official change.

What action may be needed

Configure your app's allowed IPs in the Dev Dashboard: If your app calls Shopify from static IP addresses, configure your IP allowlist based on static IPs or CIDR range. If your app's egress IP addresses are dynamic, allow your hosting provider's ASNs and list the countries your app runs in. Audit every app you've installed on stores outside your organization, and configure an allowlist on each one before January 1, 2027. For setup instructions, see Restrict API access with an IP allowlist.
